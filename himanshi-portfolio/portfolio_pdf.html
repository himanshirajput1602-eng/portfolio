Strategic Planning

CONTENT CONCEPTS

Three proactive campaigns designed to build curiosity and drive cafe footfall.
Campaign 01
THE CLOUD MATCHA RITUAL
Product Education
Hook: "Ever wondered why we whisk our matcha with bamboo instead of metal?"
Format: 3-Slide Carousel + 15s Whispering ASMR Pour Reel.
"Not all green is bitter. Watch how our ceremonial grade matcha transforms under 80 whisks into pure velvet."
Objective: Demystify ceremonial matcha and drive first-time trial orders.
Campaign 02
STUDY, SIP & SAVOR
Lifestyle & Community
Hook: "Your desk at home isn't inspiring you today. Pack your laptop—we saved you a quiet corner."
Format: Day-in-the-Life Reel + "Table for One" photo series.
"Warm lofi, cold brew with clean notes, and warm light. Sometimes focus just needs the right corner."
Objective: Position Seoul Sip as the tranquil study haven for students and remote creatives in Bhopal.
Campaign 03
SECRET MENU SUNDAYS
Word-of-Mouth Hype
Hook: "Ask the barista for 'The Minal Sunset'. Only 25 glasses made this Sunday."
Format: Countdown teaser story + secret password drop.
"Not printed on our menu board. Whisper the secret name between 3 PM & 6 PM this Sunday."
Objective: Spark viral curiosity, story shares, and rapid Sunday footfall.

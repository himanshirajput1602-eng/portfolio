WHO I AM

"Finding the authentic heartbeat of a brand, and turning everyday moments into content that resonates."

I am a third-year B.Tech Computer Science and Data Science student with an active, genuine passion for brand communication, content planning, and social media strategy. While my academic studies give me analytical discipline and a structured mindset, my creative energy is dedicated to exploring how brands build meaningful relationships through visual and verbal storytelling.

I enjoy exploring fresh creative directions and finding ways to communicate a brand's unique personality through digital media. My goal is to grow further in Content & Communication and Social Media Management, bringing fresh perspective, audience empathy, and dedicated execution to forward-thinking brands.

CONTENT CONCEPTS

Proactive campaign frameworks designed to build anticipation, strengthen brand affinity, and drive real-world engagement.

Campaign Concept 01

THE CLOUD MATCHA RITUAL

Objective Demystify ceremonial matcha for first-time cafe goers and highlight Seoul Sip's cloud foam signature.
Format 3-Part Carousel + ASMR Whispering Pour Reel (15s).
Creative Hook "Ever wondered why we whisk our matcha with bamboo instead of metal?"
"Not all green is bitter. Watch how our ceremonial grade matcha transforms under 80 whisks into pure velvet."
Intended Metric Saves, Recipe curiosity, Orders of Iced Cloud Matcha.
Campaign Concept 02

STUDY, SIP & SAVOR

Objective Position Seoul Sip as Bhopal's calmest study and creative sanctuary for students & remote workers.
Format Day-in-the-Life Reel + "Table for One" aesthetic photos.
Creative Hook "Your desk at home isn't inspiring you today. Pack your laptop—we saved you a quiet corner."
"Warm lofi, cold brew with clean notes, and warm light. Sometimes focus just needs the right corner."
Intended Metric Weekday afternoon visits, story mentions with laptops/study notes.
Campaign Concept 03

SECRET MENU SUNDAYS

Objective Create organic word-of-mouth FOMO and Sunday footfall with limited-batch experimental drinks.
Format Teaser Stories with countdown sticker + Password drop post.
Creative Hook "Ask the barista for 'The Minal Sunset'. Only 25 glasses made this Sunday."
"Not printed on our menu board. Whisper the secret name between 3 PM & 6 PM this Sunday."
Intended Metric Rapid story sharing, direct message inquiries, weekend sell-out speed.

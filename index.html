CONTENT CONCEPTS

Campaign frameworks designed to build anticipation and drive real-world engagement.

Campaign Concept 01

THE CLOUD MATCHA RITUAL

Objective Demystify ceremonial matcha and highlight Seoul Sip's cloud foam signature.
Format 3-Part Carousel + ASMR Whispering Pour Reel (15s).
Creative Hook "Ever wondered why we whisk our matcha with bamboo instead of metal?"
"Not all green is bitter. Watch how our ceremonial grade matcha transforms under 80 whisks into pure velvet."
Intended Metric Saves, Recipe curiosity, Orders of Iced Cloud Matcha.
Campaign Concept 02

STUDY, SIP & SAVOR

Objective Position Seoul Sip as Bhopal's calmest study sanctuary for students & remote workers.
Format Day-in-the-Life Reel + "Table for One" aesthetic photos.
Creative Hook "Your desk at home isn't inspiring you today. Pack your laptop—we saved you a quiet corner."
"Warm lofi, cold brew with clean notes, and warm light. Sometimes focus just needs the right corner."
Intended Metric Weekday afternoon visits, story mentions with laptops/study notes.
Campaign Concept 03

SECRET MENU SUNDAYS

Objective Create word-of-mouth FOMO and Sunday footfall with limited-batch experimental drinks.
Format Teaser Stories with countdown sticker + Password drop post.
Creative Hook "Ask the barista for 'The Minal Sunset'. Only 25 glasses made this Sunday."
"Not printed on our menu board. Whisper the secret name between 3 PM & 6 PM this Sunday."
Intended Metric Rapid story sharing, direct message inquiries, weekend sell-out speed.
